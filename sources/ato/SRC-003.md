SECTION: Conventional clothing

CONTENT:
Everyday or conventional clothing is not deductible merely because an employer requires it or it is only worn at work.

Deductions may be available for eligible compulsory uniforms, occupation-specific clothing and protective clothing.

SECTION: Driver licence

CONTENT:
The ordinary cost of obtaining or renewing a driver's licence is private, even where driving is required for work.

Additional costs for certain special licences or conditions needed to perform employment duties may be deductible.
