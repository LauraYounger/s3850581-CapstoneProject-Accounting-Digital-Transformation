SECTION: General travel deduction rule

CONTENT:
An employee can claim work-related travel expenses they incur in the course of performing their work, provided the expense is directly related to earning income and is not reimbursed.

SECTION: Private travel

CONTENT:
Normal travel between home and the regular workplace is generally private and not deductible.

Food and incidental expenses are also generally not deductible where the worker does not sleep away from home overnight.
