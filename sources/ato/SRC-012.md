SECTION: $300 work related expense threshold

CONTENT:
The $300 rule is a substantiation exception, not a deduction by itself.

The taxpayer must actually incur deductible expenses and must still be able to explain how the claim was calculated.

SECTION: Laundry up to $150

CONTENT:
A laundry claim of $150 or less can fall within a written-evidence exception, but the taxpayer still needs a reasonable basis or record showing how the amount was worked out.
