RETRIEVAL_DATE: 2026-10-10
SECTION: Connection to current employment

CONTENT:
Self-education expenses can be deductible where the study maintains or improves skills or knowledge used in the taxpayer's current employment, or is likely to increase income from those current activities.

RETRIEVAL_DATE: 2026-10-10
SECTION: New employment

CONTENT:
Study expenses are not deductible where the education is undertaken to obtain new employment or change to a different type of employment.

SECTION: Assets over $300

CONTENT:
For an eligible depreciating asset used for work-related self-education, an asset costing more than $300 is generally claimed through decline in value over its effective life rather than as an immediate full deduction.
