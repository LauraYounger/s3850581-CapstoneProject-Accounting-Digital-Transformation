SECTION: Additional running expenses

CONTENT:
A worker may claim additional running expenses caused by working from home, such as eligible energy, internet, phone and depreciating-asset costs.

Private household use is not deductible.

SECTION: Occupancy expenses

CONTENT:
Rent or mortgage-interest type occupancy expenses are only potentially deductible in limited circumstances, such as where part of the home qualifies as a place of business.

Ordinary occasional working from home does not automatically make rent deductible.
