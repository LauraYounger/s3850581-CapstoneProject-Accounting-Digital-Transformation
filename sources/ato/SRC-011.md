SECTION: Deductible gift recipient requirement

CONTENT:
A tax deduction is generally available only where the gift or donation is made to an organisation with deductible gift recipient status and the other gift conditions are met.

SECTION: Crowdfunding

CONTENT:
Payments made through crowdfunding or social-media fundraising are not automatically deductible.

Deductibility depends on whether the recipient is an eligible deductible gift recipient and the payment satisfies the gift rules.
