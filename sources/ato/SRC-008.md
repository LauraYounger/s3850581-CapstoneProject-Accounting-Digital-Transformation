SECTION: Prescription eyewear

CONTENT:
Ordinary prescription glasses and contact lenses are private expenses even when they are needed while working.

SECTION: Protective eyewear

CONTENT:
Protective glasses, including eligible sunglasses or safety eyewear, may be deductible where they protect against a real and likely work-related risk.

Only the work-related portion is deductible.
