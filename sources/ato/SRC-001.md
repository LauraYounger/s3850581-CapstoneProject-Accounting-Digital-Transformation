SECTION: Cents per kilometre method

CONTENT: 
The cents per kilometre method allows a deduction  based on eligible work-related kilometres travelled.

The method has a maximum of 5,000 work-related kilometres per car for the income year.

CONTENT:
The taxpayer must be able to demonstrate how the number of work-related kilometres was calculated.

Individual receipts for each car expense are not required when using the cents per kilometre method.
