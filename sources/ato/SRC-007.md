SECTION: Gym and fitness expenses

CONTENT:
Gym fees and fitness-related expenses are generally private and not deductible.

Limited exceptions may apply where the required physical activity is sufficiently connected with the employee's duties.

SECTION: Sun protection

CONTENT:
Eligible sun-protection products may be deductible where the employee is exposed to the sun for prolonged periods while performing work duties.

Any private use needs to be excluded from the claim.
